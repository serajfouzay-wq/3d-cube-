CABINET — how to run it on Windows
==================================

STEP 1 — Unzip everything first
-------------------------------
Right-click the zip file you downloaded and choose "Extract All...".
Pick somewhere easy, like your Desktop.

This step matters. If you open the zip and double-click Cabinet.exe
from INSIDE the zip window, it will not work. Extract it first.


STEP 2 — Open the app
---------------------
Go into the extracted folder and double-click:

    Cabinet.exe


STEP 3 — Windows will show a blue warning. This is expected.
------------------------------------------------------------
You will probably see:

    "Windows protected your PC"

This appears because the app has not been through Microsoft's paid
code-signing process. It is not a virus warning and it does not mean
anything is wrong. Every unsigned app shows it.

To continue:
    1. Click "More info"
    2. Click "Run anyway"

You only have to do this the first time.


STEP 4 — Set yourself up (2 minutes)
------------------------------------
1. Click "Settings" in the left sidebar.
2. Type your company name, and add your logo if you have one.
   This is what appears at the top of every proposal you create.
3. Check the "Library folder" — this is where Cabinet will keep the
   organised copies of your documents. The default is fine.
4. Click "Save settings".


STEP 5 — Try the file scanner
-----------------------------
1. Click "Filing desk" in the sidebar.
2. Click "Choose a folder" and pick a folder that has some PDFs or
   Word documents in it. Your Downloads folder is a good test.
3. Watch it read them and work out which company each one belongs to.
4. Anything it is unsure about appears in a review list where you can
   correct it.
5. Click "File ... into library" when you are happy.

YOUR ORIGINAL FILES ARE NOT MOVED OR DELETED. Cabinet copies them.
You can change this in Settings if you would rather it move them.


STEP 6 — Write a proposal
-------------------------
1. Click "Write a proposal".
2. Type what you want in normal English, for example:
       a proposal for Acme Ltd covering a 12 week website rebuild
3. Click "Generate draft".
4. Edit any wording you like, then type in your prices.
5. Click "PDF" to save a finished document.

NOTE ON PRICES: Cabinet will never invent a price for you. Every
amount has to be typed in by you, and the PDF button stays greyed out
until every line has a price. This is deliberate.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- Nothing happens when you double-click Cabinet.exe
      You are probably still inside the zip. Go back to Step 1.

- "Choose a folder" does nothing
      Try a smaller folder first. Scanning a whole drive takes a while.

- A document was filed under the wrong company
      Open "Library", find it, and you can remove it from the index.
      The file on your disk is never touched.

Everything happens on your own computer. No documents are uploaded.
